# Test plate for ultrasonic inspection trials

Fabrication specification. One plate. All dimensions in millimetres.

Contact: Harshal Dhandrut

---

## 1. What this is for

A calibration and demonstration piece for ultrasonic thickness testing. It carries
deliberate weld defects on the front face and two deliberately thinned pockets machined
into the back face. The thin pockets must not be visible from the front. That is the
whole point of the piece: it is used to prove that an instrument finds metal loss that
the eye cannot see.

Please do not repair, dress back or tidy up any of the defects listed below. They are
wanted exactly as described.

## 4. Front face: three weld beads

Three straight bead on plate runs. These are **not** joining two pieces. They are beads
laid on the surface of the single plate.

| Bead | Position | Length | Runs from | Height above plate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Y = 60 | 500 | X = 50 to X = 550 | about 3.5 |
| B | Y = 150 | 500 | X = 50 to X = 550 | about 3.5 |
| C | Y = 240 | 500 | X = 50 to X = 550 | about 3.5 |

Process is your choice, MIG or stick, whichever gives a normal looking bead with visible
ripple. Bead width around 10 to 14 is expected. Position tolerance plus or minus 5.

---

## 5. Front face: four deliberate weld defects

These are faults on purpose. A good welder will find this an odd request. They are
wanted, and the piece is useless without them.

| Ref | Bead | Centred at | What is wanted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B001 | A | X = 350 | Stop the run for **60** and restart, leaving a clear gap in the bead with bare plate showing in it |
| B002 | B | X = 190 | Run a grinder along the lower toe of the bead for **80**, cutting a groove about **1.5 deep** into the plate beside the bead. The groove must be in the plate, not in the bead |
| B003 | B | X = 400 | **Five** pores or dimples about **2 diameter** in the face of the bead, within any 100 of length. Drilling them is acceptable |
| B004 | C | X = 280 | One hole **6 diameter** burned or drilled right through the bead and the plate |

---

## 6. Back face: two machined pockets. This is the critical work

Two flat bottomed recesses machined into the **back** face only. Nothing may show on the
front face.

| Ref | Centre | Diameter | Metal to be LEFT under the pocket |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | X = 460, Y = 105 | 40 | **3.0** |
| P2 | X = 140, Y = 195 | 40 | **4.0** |

**Read the last column carefully. It is the metal remaining, not the depth of cut.**

On a 6 mm plate, leaving 3.0 means cutting about 3.0 deep. Leaving 4.0 means cutting
about 2.0 deep. These are opposite instructions and getting them the wrong way round
makes the plate useless. If anything is unclear here, please call before cutting.

Requirements:

- **Flat bottom.** An end mill, not a grinder and not a dish. The floor must be parallel
  to the plate faces. An instrument reads the thickness directly under one small point,
  and a curved floor gives a different answer at every point
- **Square to the faces**, within reason
- Position tolerance plus or minus 5, not critical
- Edges of the pocket may be left sharp

### What actually matters, and what does not

Hitting 3.0 and 4.0 exactly is **not** critical. Anything within about plus or minus 0.3
is fine.

**Measuring what you achieved and writing it down is critical.** The piece is used to
check an instrument against a known truth, and your measurement is the known truth. A
pocket that came out at 3.17 is perfectly good as long as the sheet says 3.17.

Please measure and report, to 0.05 where you can:

1. Plate thickness on clear metal next to each pocket
2. Depth of each pocket
3. The remaining metal, which is 1 minus 2

A vernier depth gauge or a micrometer is fine. Write the numbers on a slip of paper or
message them, with the pocket reference P1 and P2.

---

## 7. Front face surface, over the pockets

An ultrasonic probe sits on the **front** face directly above each pocket, so those areas
need to be smooth enough to make contact.

In a **60 diameter** area on the front face directly opposite P1 and P2:

- Remove loose mill scale, rust and spatter
- Lightly linish or flap disc it so a probe can sit flat
- Do not dish it, do not grind it thin, do not polish it

The pockets have been positioned in the clear bands between the weld beads so there is
room to do this without touching a bead. Please keep the beads clear of this work.

---

## 8. Leave alone

One corner of the plate, roughly the last **60 x 60** at the X = 600, Y = 300 corner,
should be left bare and untouched with no oil, so it develops surface rust naturally. Do
not clean it up.
